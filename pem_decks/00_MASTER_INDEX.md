---

## Design System
- **Color Palette**: Navy primary (#0B1D3A), Teal accent (#007B83), Coral alerts (#E84D4D), Amber warnings (#F0A500)
- **Typography**: Calibri throughout (clean, professional, universally available)
- **Layout**: Widescreen 16:9 (13.333 × 7.5 inches)
- **Components**: Title slide, section dividers, objectives, content, tables, algorithms, key points, two-column comparisons, clinical cases, pitfalls, takeaways, references, closing slide
- **Footer**: Consistent "PEM Fellow Teaching Library" branding with slide numbers

## How to Use This Library

### For Fellow Teaching
- Use individual decks for 30-60 minute teaching sessions
- Presenter notes provide spoken teaching points for each slide
- Cases can be used for bedside teaching or case-based learning

### For Board Preparation
- Start with Deck 30 (Board Review) for a rapid overview
- Use individual decks to deep-dive weak areas
- The "Pitfalls" slides in every deck highlight board-tested errors

### For Academic Presentations
- Decks are formatted for conference-level presentation
- Consistent design system works for grand rounds, M&M, and departmental talks
- Customize content for your specific audience and institution
